CEFR: A2 – B1

S3: Can, Could & Be Able To

📚 1. Grammar Explanation（文法の説明）

1) "Can" expresses present ability or possibility.
Use can + base verb to say what someone is able to do right now.

「can」は現在の能力や可能性を表します。can＋動詞の原形で、今できることを表します。

2) "Could" expresses past ability (general) or polite requests.
Use could + base verb for something you were generally able to do in the past. For a specific achievement in the past, use was/were able to.

「could」は過去の一般的な能力を表します。過去の特定の達成には was/were able to を使います。

3) "Be able to" works in all tenses — can, could, and will cannot.
This is the key reason to learn be able to: it fills the gaps where modal verbs don't work.

「be able to」はすべての時制で使えます。これが be able to を学ぶ最大の理由です。
Time Can / Could Be Able To 日本語
Present can speak am / is / are able to speak 話せる（現在）
Past (general) could speak was / were able to speak 話せた（過去・一般）
Past (specific achievement) ❌ not used was / were able to speak （その時）話せた（達成）
Future ❌ not used will be able to speak 話せるようになる（未来）
Negative (present) can't speak am / is / are not able to speak 話せない
Negative (past — general) couldn't speak was / were not able to speak 話せなかった（一般）
Negative (past — specific attempt) ❌ not used wasn't / weren't able to speak （その時）話せなかった（試みたが失敗）

⚠️ Key difference: affirmative past

⚠️ 肯定文での重要な違い：
Sentence Meaning 日本語
When I was young, I could run fast. General past ability 若い頃、速く走れた（一般的な能力）
The door was locked, but I was able to open it. Specific achievement 鍵がかかっていたが、開けることができた（達成）

⚠️ Key difference: negative past

⚠️ 否定文での重要な違い：
Sentence Meaning 日本語
I couldn't swim as a child. General past inability 子どもの頃、泳げなかった（一般的な不能）
I tried hard but wasn't able to finish the race. Specific failed attempt 頑張ったが、レースを完走できなかった（試みたが失敗）
💡 否定文では couldn't の方が一般的に自然です。wasn't / weren't able to は特定の試みが失敗した場合に使います。

✏️ 2. Short Example Sentences（短い例文）

• She can speak three languages.

彼女は3か国語を話せます。

• I can't find my keys anywhere.

どこを探しても鍵が見つかりません。

• When he was a child, he could swim very well.

子どもの頃、彼はとても上手に泳げました。

• We were able to finish the project on time.

私たちはプロジェクトを時間通りに終わらせることができました。

• After more practice, you will be able to play the piano well.

もっと練習すれば、ピアノが上手に弾けるようになります。

• He wasn't able to attend the meeting yesterday.

彼は昨日、会議に出席できませんでした。

• Are you able to help me with this report?

このレポートを手伝っていただけますか？

📝 3. Longer Example Sentences（長い例文）

• My grandmother could cook amazing food when she was young, but now she isn't able to stand for long periods.

祖母は若い頃、素晴らしい料理を作れましたが、今は長時間立っていることができません。

• I studied hard all week, and I was able to pass the exam on Friday.

一週間ずっと一生懸命勉強して、金曜日の試験に合格することができました。

• If you keep practising every day, you will be able to have a natural conversation in English within six months.

毎日練習し続ければ、6か月以内に英語で自然な会話ができるようになります。

• The instructions were very complicated, but after reading them twice, she was able to put the furniture together by herself.

説明書はとても複雑でしたが、2回読んだ後、彼女は一人で家具を組み立てることができました。

• He can understand spoken English well, but he isn't able to write it fluently yet.

彼は英語のリスニングは得意ですが、まだ流暢に書くことができません。

🧩 4. Practice Exercises

Exercise A – Choose the correct option

1) When I was five, I ______ ride a bike.
a) can   b) could   c) will be able to
b) could — general past ability
2) The traffic was terrible, but we ______ arrive on time.
a) could   b) were able to   c) will be able to
b) were able to — specific past achievement
3) After this course, you ______ write professional emails in English.
a) can   b) could   c) will be able to
c) will be able to — future ability
4) She ______ come to the party last night because she was sick.
a) couldn't   b) can't   c) won't be able to
a) couldn't — past inability
5) ______ you ______ help me move this table?
a) Can / —   b) Will / be able to   c) Were / able to
a) Can / — — polite present request

Exercise B – Complete the sentence

1) I studied for months and finally ______ (pass) the driving test.
was able to pass
2) He ______ (not / speak) Spanish when he moved to Madrid, but he learned quickly.
couldn't speak / was not able to speak
3) If you practise more, you ______ (play) the guitar at the concert.
will be able to play
4) My grandfather ______ (run) very fast when he was young.
could run
5) ______ you ______ (attend) the meeting tomorrow?
Will you be able to attend

📖 5. Short Reading Text（読解文）

A New Language, A New Life

When Yuki moved to Canada three years ago, she couldn't speak English at all. She wasn't able to order food at a restaurant or ask for directions on the street. Every day felt like a challenge.

But Yuki didn't give up. She studied every evening after work and practised with her neighbours whenever she could. After six months, she was able to have simple conversations. After a year, she could speak English comfortably in most situations.

Now, Yuki works as a customer service representative at a busy office. She can speak English fluently and is even able to help new Japanese employees who are just starting to learn. She often tells them: "It's hard at first, but if you keep trying, you will be able to do it too."

新しい言語、新しい人生

ユキが3年前にカナダに引っ越してきたとき、英語はまったく話せませんでした。レストランで注文することも、道を尋ねることもできませんでした。毎日が挑戦の連続でした。

しかし、ユキはあきらめませんでした。仕事の後は毎晩勉強し、できる限り隣人と練習しました。6か月後には、簡単な会話ができるようになりました。1年後には、ほとんどの状況で快適に英語を話せるようになっていました。

今、ユキは忙しいオフィスでカスタマーサービス担当として働いています。英語を流暢に話せるようになり、学び始めたばかりの日本人の新入社員を助けることさえできます。彼女はよくこう言います。「最初は大変だけど、諦めなければあなたもきっとできるようになる。」

❓ 6. Reading Comprehension Questions（読解問題）

1) What couldn't Yuki do when she first arrived in Canada?
ユキがカナダに到着した当初、何ができませんでしたか。
She couldn't speak English, order food at a restaurant, or ask for directions.
2) How did Yuki practise her English outside of studying?
ユキは勉強以外でどのように英語を練習しましたか。
She practised with her neighbours whenever she could.
3) How long did it take Yuki to have simple conversations?
ユキが簡単な会話ができるようになるまでどのくらいかかりましたか。
After six months.
4) What is Yuki able to do now that she couldn't do before?
ユキが今できるようになったことで、以前できなかったことは何ですか。
She can speak English fluently and help new Japanese employees who are learning English.
5) What does Yuki tell new employees about learning English?
ユキは新入社員に英語学習についてどのようなことを伝えていますか。
She tells them it is hard at first, but if they keep trying, they will be able to do it too.

🎯 7. Summary（まとめ）

• Use can for present ability and can't for present inability.

現在の能力には can、現在の不能には can't を使います。

• Use could for general past ability and couldn't for general past inability.

過去の一般的な能力には could、一般的な不能には couldn't を使います。

• Use was / were able to for a specific past achievement — not could.

過去の特定の達成には was / were able to を使います（couldは不可）。

• Use will be able to for future ability — can has no future form.

未来の能力には will be able to を使います（canに未来形はありません）。

• Be able to works in all tenses; can and could do not.

be able to はすべての時制で使えますが、can と could は限られた時制にしか使えません。
